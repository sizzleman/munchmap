# OpenStreetMap extracts: ODbL 1.0

The `*.json` files in this folder are extracts of restaurants, cafés and fast-food places from
**OpenStreetMap**, © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the
**Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0**: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
and https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright

They're generated by `scripts/snapshot-osm.ts` and kept **separate** from munchmap's own community data
(`data/`, CC BY-SA 4.0). If you reuse these files, keep them under the ODbL and keep the attribution.
Fix wrong restaurant info at the source on https://www.openstreetmap.org, and the next snapshot picks it up.
